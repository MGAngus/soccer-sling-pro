Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. The game saves automatically after every level. Progress isn't synced between devices, and deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Why are there ads?

Soccer Sling PRO is free to play. An ad is shown after every fifth level you complete, when you continue from the results screen. There are no ads while you aim and shoot.

How do I get an extra ball?

If you run out of balls before every opponent is down, the Red card! screen offers an extra ball for watching a short video ad. Watch the ad to the end and the level continues with one more ball. The offer is available once per attempt, whenever an ad is ready.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you live in a region where consent is required, such as the EU, tap the ⓘ button on the title screen and then Privacy settings. To choose whether apps may track you, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Tap the ⓘ button on the title screen and then Report an ad, or email me at the address above. Tell me what the ad showed and roughly when you saw it.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the sound button on the title screen, or open the pause menu during a level.

I'm stuck on a level

Try a different angle or less power, and tap the screen mid-flight to use a trick ball's special ability: the Cannon fires off like a rocket, the Hat-Trick splits into three and the Bomb explodes. Fireworks crates set off chain reactions. Any win unlocks the next level, and every ball you don't need is worth bonus points toward more stars.

How do I unlock the next chapter?

Win the chapter's final against the Coach: levels 10, 20, 30 and 40 open the next chapter.
